What we're making

What's in the trove.

OctoShaper

Available for Unity

Node-based procedural generation for Unity. Wire up a few nodes, drag a slider, and watch towers, forests and castle walls rebuild right in your scene.

  • 200+ nodes for scattering, splines, meshes and more
  • The same graph in the editor and in your game
  • One-time purchase on the Unity Asset Store
